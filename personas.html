RIZZIO PERSONAS

The identity system

This page separates the person at the source, the central Rizzio character, and the identities Rizzio performs or becomes. They are not interchangeable. Steven is the first character in this system. Rizzio is the character Steven created and developed—not simply another name for Steven, and not a claim that every lyric is a literal account of Steven.

The order follows the catalogue and the evidence: real-life source → Rizzio → distinct transformations and roles. Later songs and artwork deepen the identities; they must not be projected backwards as if every detail existed from the start.

How the identities relate

1 · Steven — The person and first character in the canon. His real experiences, roles and observations can supply the raw material.

2 · Rizzio — Steven’s created character: distinct enough to be narrated, observed and shown alongside Steven, rather than being collapsed into him.

3 · Personas and transformations — Sir Rizz, SuperRizzio, Dirty Old Man in a Raincoat and The Executive Seducer are not interchangeable labels. Each has its own trigger, role, tone and evidence in the chronology.

4 · Project X and the wider world — Later titles and political imagery can expand Rizzio’s mythology, but they do not turn the collective into a solo project or retroactively change earlier identities.

5 - Rizzio's Moody Moods: — “The Street Charmer”, “The Alley Lawyer”, “The Crude Confessor”, “The Weekly Player”, “The Funk Technician”, “The Evaluator”, “The Conflicted Romantic”, “The Rejector”, “The Sexual Satirist”, “The Nursery-Rhyme Deviant”, “The Dismissive Historian”, “The Aggressor”, “The Sexual Ringmaster” and “The Disdainful Comparator” are analytical labels. “The Rizla” also is not listed as a separate persona: the available lyric uses “The Rizla, the Rizza, the Riddla, the Razor” as a rhyming sequence, which is not enough on its own to establish four distinct identities. “Game Show Host” is a track-specific role in #060, and not a sufficiently established recurring persona.
