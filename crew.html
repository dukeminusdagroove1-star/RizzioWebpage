CREW MAP, PERSONA CONSTELLATION & AFFILIATES

🧭 THE PEOPLE, CREWS & CONNECTIONS

Each entry uses the same Role / Key Tracks / Threads format. Track numbers follow the master catalogue chronology. The illustrations are symbolic emblems, not claims about anyone’s physical appearance. Early associates, later aliases, collaborators and formal Project X membership are kept distinct: a name appearing in a lyric does not by itself make that person a Project X member.

👥 SABA And CKW

  • Role: The named early crew/social circle celebrated in the 1993 material; a distinct formation from the later Project X collective.
  • Key Tracks: “SABA And CKW” (#006); “Down The Boulevard” (#010) provides adjacent early-circle context.
  • Threads: The closing roll-call names Steven, Ashley, Bobby, Andrew, Cornell, Kevin and Winston; the song also brings in Ebony Man and Star. Its sketches of people’s habits and interests make this a character map, not merely a name-check. Do not retroactively rename this crew Project X.

🧠 Steven

  • Role: Foundational character and early point-of-view figure; the character history that precedes and develops into Rizzio.
  • Key Tracks: “SABA And CKW” (#006); “Down The Boulevard” (#010); further early-era appearances.
  • Threads: In #006 he is the organiser/narrating presence moving between friends and encounters. Keep Steven distinct from Rizzio as the created identity that develops from him; later continuity does not erase the earlier character stage.

🎙️ Rizzio aka Sir Rizz aka Rizzio The Executive Seducer

  • Role: Central artist/narrator and later identity; performer and connective voice across solo material and the Project X collective.
  • Key Tracks: “Journey Through Da Lon-Town” (#048); Project X material (#109–#120); catalogue-wide where credited or narrated.
  • Threads: The voice links the early social world to the later collective, but Rizzio’s solo precursor tracks are not automatically group recordings. The persona system grows around this voice; the crew page treats actual collaborators separately from Rizzio’s fictional/persona branches.

🌀 Ashley aka PH 2 aka Gemini aka Da Infamous Infrared

  • Role: One connected identity-history: Ashley is the early name; Gemini is the later-known identity, also identified as Infrared and PH 2 in the catalogue.
  • Key Tracks: “SABA And CKW” (#006); “Journey Through Da Lon-Town” (#048); “Aroundaway (Featuring P.H.2 Aka Infra-Red)” (#073); “Ashley’s Goin’ Church!” (#078); later Project X references.
  • Threads: The early Ashley material is a precursor, not proof that the later Project X formation already existed in 1993. The catalogue explicitly links Ashley aka Gemini and uses Infrared / PH 2 as aliases later on.

🪒 Bobby “Razor” Razak

  • Role: Early friend/crew associate, explicitly named in the SABA & CKW roll-call and later recalled by name.
  • Key Tracks: “SABA And CKW” (#006); “Journey Through Da Lon-Town” (#048); later recollection in “Ashley’s Goin’ Church!” (#078).
  • Threads: The #006 roll-call names Bobby; #048 gives the fuller form “Bobby, Razor Razak”. The later line that Bobby left for the States adds a biographical thread. He belongs to the early SABA & CKW history.

🧍 Andrew

  • Role: Named member of the early SABA & CKW circle; characterised in the early material through his responses and interests.
  • Key Tracks: “SABA And CKW” (#006); “Down The Boulevard” (#010).
  • Threads: The early track gives him a specific place in the group’s roll-call and contrasts his interests with those of Steven, Ashley, Bobby and Cornell. The available evidence supports an early associated friendship group.

🎷 Cornell 'Cornelius' aka Cornell K aka Psychoanalysizer

  • Role: Early SABA & CKW associate; Cornell is also known as Cornell 'Cornelius' aka Cornell K aka Psychoanalysizer in the catalogue.
  • Key Tracks: “SABA And CKW” (#006); “Down The Boulevard” (#010); "End Of The World (Not The)" (#027); “Projex In FX Iz PX” (#100).
  • Threads: The early material sketches his style and musical taste; Then he plays a major role as Rizzio's 'Psychoanalysizer' and confidante in "End Of The World (Not The)" and the following U.S.(#037); Remix, then “Projex In FX Iz PX” later names Cornell K. This continuity links the early SABA & CKW circle to later references without automatically assigning him Project X membership.

🧠 Kevin aka Penfold

  • Role: Named early SABA & CKW associate; Kevin is also known as Penfold.
  • Key Tracks: “SABA And CKW” (#006).
  • Threads: The song gives Kevin a distinct personality tag and includes him in the final roll-call. Later, Penfold appears in “Pointless Niggaz” and in the Danger Mouse wordplay; Penfold is Kevin, connecting the early crew name to the later reference.

🧍 Winston

  • Role: Named early SABA & CKW associate.
  • Key Tracks: “SABA And CKW” (#006).
  • Threads: Winston appears in the closing list of the crew. The current evidence establishes his place in that early circle, but does not provide enough to assign a later collective role.

🕴️ Ebony Man

  • Role: Named associate appearing within the early SABA & CKW narrative.
  • Key Tracks: “SABA And CKW” (#006).
  • Threads: The lyric gives him a brief descriptive moment alongside Andrew. He is part of the song’s social world.

⭐ Star

  • Role: Named character in the early SABA & CKW story; involved in the interpersonal episode narrated in the track.
  • Key Tracks: “SABA And CKW” (#006).
  • Threads: Star is more than a passing name: the song includes an encounter and a reaction involving Cornell and Ashley. The source supports a role in this episode, not a formal crew title.

🚇 “Journey Through Da Lon-Town” — the four-person catalyst

  • Role: A solo precursor/catalyst that names Rizzio, Stone, Yinka and Gemini together and welcomes Project X.
  • Key Tracks: “Journey Through Da Lon-Town” (#048).
  • Threads: The four are described as “bad bwoys”; the track also names Bobby “Razor” Razak and sends the group through London geography. It is a crucial origin-point, but should not be read as proof that every later member or the fully operating collective was already in place at this exact moment.

🪨 Stone Alias Astonejeh Dafrilla

  • Role: Core Project X performer and creative partner; later credited as President Stone and given his own vocal sections.
  • Key Tracks: “Journey Through Da Lon-Town” (#048); Project X material (#109–#120); later Project X-era tracks including “Metaphors 4 Betta Scores” (#97).
  • Threads: Stone’s voice and named contributions help turn the precursor into a working collective. The “President Stone” label is a later role/title within the catalogue, not a reason to rewrite earlier chronology.

🎛️ Yinka The Thinka

  • Role: Core Project X performer and project/production figure, with recurring vocal and selector references.
  • Key Tracks: “Journey Through Da Lon-Town” (#048); Project X material (#109–#120); “Metaphors 4 Betta Scores” (#97) and other later group tracks.
  • Threads: The catalogue explicitly calls Yinka the project producer and gives him a recurring role in group performance. His partnership with Stone and Rizzio is central to the later collective, while the chronology still begins with the earlier precursor.

💥 General Flexus Of Texas

  • Role: Named Project X performer with credited vocal contributions in the group’s foundational material.
  • Key Tracks: Project X material, especially “Bad Lyrics” (#109) and other tracks #109–#120.
  • Threads: The track annotations assign him spoken/rap sections, making him an identifiable participant rather than a vague affiliate. His membership belongs to the Project X era.

🎚️ DJ Def K

  • Role: Named Project X member/DJ identity within the later collective.
  • Key Tracks: Project X material (#109–#120); later Project X roll-calls.
  • Threads: DJ Def is named alongside Yinka, Stone and Infrared in the group’s recurring roll-call. The DJ role is part of the collective identity.

♠️ Project X — the later collective

  • Role: A group/collective in its own right, not simply another name for Rizzio or a solo-album label.
  • Key Tracks: Project X LP material (#109–#120); earlier catalyst “Journey Through Da Lon-Town” (#048); group appearances include “This Goes Out To The Lon-Town” (#077).
  • Threads: The later roll-calls name Yinka, Stone, Infrared, General Flex and DJ Def, alongside Rizzio as the central voice. The collective is described as “bad, rough, rugged, and rude”. The working group is established by approximately August 1995. SABA & CKW remains a distinct earlier crew, connected through selected people rather than identical membership.

  • Threads: Kandace is called out in “Bad Lyrics”; Juliet is called out in “Ghetto Street Stuff”. Their vocalist status places them in the Project X orbit as collaborators, without recasting them as core members of the collective.

🎤 Homies, groupies, fans & unnamed affiliates

  • Threads: These are collective roles, not a single named gang. The songs use them to stage fame, social proof, performance and the wider orbit around Rizzio/Project X. Unnamed people should not be given invented identities or treated as confirmed members.

🎧 Too $hort

  • Role: External musical reference / artistic kin, not a member of Rizzio’s crew or Project X.
  • Key Tracks: Referenced in the catalogue’s rap/bravado context, including “Big Booty Bitches, Slutty Slags And Horny Hoes” (#044).
  • Threads: The lyrical comparison signals transatlantic rap kinship and influence. A shout-out or claim of shared style is not membership, so Too $hort is documented here as an outside reference rather than folded into the crew.

📍 Gemini, Penfold & Cornelius — linked identities

  • Role: A named trio in “Pointless Niggaz”: Gemini, Penfold and Cornelius. Penfold is Kevin; Cornelius is Cornell.
  • Key Tracks: “Pointless Niggaz” (#053); “SABA And CKW” (#006); “Projex In FX Iz PX” (#101).
  • Threads: This lyric groups three established identities in one scene: Gemini, Kevin under the name Penfold, and Cornell under the name Cornelius. The aliases clarify continuity across the catalogue.
